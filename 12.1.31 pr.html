.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the claim of a fundus or homo by condictio. A parcel of land or a slave is a determinate thing, and the passage discusses the procedural consequence after litis contestatio: restoration of everything the claimant would have had if performance had occurred then. Thus it shows recovery of a certum by condictio.
