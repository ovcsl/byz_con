.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the last scenario: the thief himself, or the thief’s/fugitive’s accomplice, has received the indicium-payment from the owner. The claim concerns restitution of what was paid in connection with theft or complicity, not vindication of a specific thing as still owned by the claimant. It is a personal condictio grounded in delict/enrichment, so the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the last one: the thief himself, or the thief’s/fugitive’s associate, receives money from the owner for giving information. That is framed as an illicit or recoverable payment connected with informing, not as money advanced on credit to be repaid in kind. There is no coinciding intention of mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

The recovery concerns the datum, i.e. the specific payment or thing handed over as the reward, not an unliquidated incertum. This is a determinate object claimable by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
