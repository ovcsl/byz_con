.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the explicit statement that, where the hereditatis or bonorum possessor defends the inheritance, an indebitum solutum may be condicted. The object is what was paid though not due; Roman condictio indebiti treats the paid certum as recoverable by condictio. Thus the passage shows a determinate paid object claimable by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
