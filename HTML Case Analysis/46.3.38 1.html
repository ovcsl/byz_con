.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the debtor’s condictio against the recipient of the coins, with the wife analogy treated as the same remedial pattern. Although the text says the coins do not become the recipient’s, the claim arises from a wrongful/ineffective payment and, in the Titius case, is expressly a condictio furtiva. It is therefore a personal condictio, not a vindicatory claim as owner of a specific thing. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the last explicit one: the debtor pays the wife on the husband’s order, and the debtor has a condictio against the woman because the coins do not become hers. The transaction is framed as a payment/donation arrangement, not as money advanced by both parties as a mutuum repayable in kind. No coinciding intention to create a loan for consumption appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
