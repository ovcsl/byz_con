.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the final alternative: if the dominus does not ratify, the payer must bring the condictio against the procurator. The object is id quod indebitum solutum sit, i.e. the determinate thing or sum paid but not owed, claimed back by condictio. This is a recovery of a certum arising from an undue payment, not an ownership claim or defective-credit case. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
