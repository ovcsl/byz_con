.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio-passage is the final clause: the woman, after or before condemnation, may condicere against the person who delegated her for what she has lost or, if no loss yet, for release. This is a personal restitutionary/indemnity claim arising from intercession and delegation, not a claim to a specific thing as her subsisting property. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the final one: the woman, quasi debitrix delegata, may condicere against the person who delegated her for her loss or for liberation. This concerns delegation and intercession, not a giving of money or fungibles as a mutuum repayable in kind. The parties’ coinciding intention to create a loan for consumption is not shown; conclusion: not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the final one: the woman, delegated as quasi debtor, may condicere against the person who delegated her, either for what she has lost or for liberation if she has not yet suffered loss. The object is framed as indemnification or release from an obligation, not as a fixed sum or specifically identified thing pursued as a certum. Thus the certum element is not satisfied.
