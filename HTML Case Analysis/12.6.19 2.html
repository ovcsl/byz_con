.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the mistaken payment of coins not owed, some belonging to the payer and some to another. The text expressly says he condicts half of the sum, not the specific coin-bodies. This is a personal restitutionary claim for a determinate amount, not a vindicatory claim based on subsisting ownership. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the mistaken payment of coins thought to be owed, partly owned by the payer and partly by another. The passage treats the transfer as solutio indebiti, not as money credited to the recipient for later repayment in tantundem. No coinciding intention to create a mutuum appears. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.

Although a fixed monetary amount is discussed, the passage turns on a defective payment because the coins were not wholly the payer’s. This falls under the excluded defective-giving constellation, so the criterion is not satisfied.
