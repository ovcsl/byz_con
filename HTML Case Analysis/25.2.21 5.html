.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe only explicit condictio-passage is the reference to condictio furtiva, used as an analogy for an action arising from delict yet pursuing the thing. In Roman law that condictio lies from the theft-delict against the thief, even though the owner may retain title. It is therefore a personal delictual/enrichment claim, not a vindicatory claim based on subsisting ownership. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
