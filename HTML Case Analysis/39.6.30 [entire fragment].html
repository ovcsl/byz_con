.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: the donor mortis causa, on repentance, has a condictio or utilis actio. The claim rests on revocation of the mortis causa donation and restitution of what was given, not on an assertion that the donor remains owner and vindicates the specific thing. This is a personal restitutionary claim, so the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the donor’s condictio after a donatio mortis causa revoked by repentance. The transaction is expressly a gift in contemplation of death, not a credit of fungibles to be repaid in kind by mutual intention. Therefore the required consensus for a mutuum is absent.
