.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the father’s attempted condictio of what was given dotis causa for an emancipated daughter, with the condictio arising only if the intended marriage-cause failed and being extinguished by her marriage. The claim is framed as restitution of a dowry-giving for causa non secuta, not as recovery of a specific thing because it remains the father’s property. It is therefore a personal restitutionary claim, not a vindicatory condictio. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the father’s attempted condicere of what was given dotis causa for an emancipated daughter. The text treats the transfer as a dowry-related giving, with possible recovery only if the matrimonial cause fails, not as money or fungibles advanced as a mutuum to be repaid in kind. There is no coinciding intention to create a loan for consumption. Conclusion: not satisfied.

The object is expressed as quae dotis causa data sunt, i.e. the specific dowry assets or amount given, not an open-ended incertum. The passage therefore discusses recovery of a determinate object by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
