.3.291Person Z is enriched at the expense of person Y, who should have had property from X. 1AktionThe decisive scenario is the final dowry case: the woman recovers by condictio the things delivered through the slave or filius familias, and by condictio incerti obtains release from the dowry promise. The dominus or pater acquires the dowry benefit through the dependent person, while the text denies ordinary contractual liability for risk or culpa; the enrichment remains identifiable as the delivered res or the acquired promise. This is a three-person enrichment case against the enriched acquirer, so the criterion is satisfied.

The dowry was promised or delivered through a slave or filius familias, with the master or father only later ratifying, not by prior iussum or praepositio. The condictio is therefore considered on the master’s or father’s side through the dependant’s act. Conclusion: satisfied.

.5.4102In rem domini vel patris versum (applied to the benefit of the master or father)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the recovery by condictio of dowry property delivered to the slave or filius familias, together with the incerti condictio for release from the promise. The passage states that an obligation taken through the slave is acquired by the master, and treats the dowry delivery through the dependant as effective only with the master’s or father’s ratification. Thus the benefit is located in the master’s or father’s legal sphere, not merely in a peculium. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
