.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the heir’s claim against the freedman who received money to build a monument and failed to do so. The claim arises from receipt of a sum for an unfulfilled purpose, not from the heir asserting subsisting ownership of specific coins or reclaiming them in specie. It is a personal restitutionary claim for the amount received. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the heir giving money to the freedman so that a monument be made, followed by the freedman’s failure to make it. The giving is directed to performance of that purpose, not to a credit of money to be repaid in the same quantity and kind. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio: the heir pays the freedman a fixed sum for making the monument, and the freedman fails to do so. The object recoverable by condictio is the money paid, described as a certa summa, hence a determinate certum grounded in numeratio. The criterion is satisfied.
