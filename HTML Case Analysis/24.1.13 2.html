.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the delivery to someone so that he build on his own land, where condici ei id non potest is stated. The passage treats the transfer as falling under donation, not as a claim to recover a specific thing still owned by the giver. It is therefore not a vindicatory condictio based on subsisting ownership. Conclusion: not satisfied.

There is therefore no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit passage: one who received something to build on his own land cannot have 'id' condicted from him. The condictio is directed to recovery of the thing or amount received, even though recovery is denied because the transfer is treated as a donation. That is a determinate object of the giving, not an incertum or ownership-based claim. Conclusion: satisfied.
