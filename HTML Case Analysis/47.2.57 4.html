.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the explicit condictio in which the tutor and curator furiosi may condicere rem furtivam in the names of those represented. Although they act in the owner’s place, the claim is the condictio for a stolen thing, arising from furtum. In Roman law that condictio is delictual/personal, not a vindicatory claim based on subsisting ownership. Conclusion: not satisfied.
