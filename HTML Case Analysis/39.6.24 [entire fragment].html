.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive scenario is the explicit condici where a debt was released by acceptilatio mortis causa and the donor later recovers. The claim arises because the former obligation has been transformed into a condictio after the causa mortis fails, not because the claimant asserts ownership of a specific thing. It is a personal restitutionary claim for the debt-value, not a vindicatory substitute. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the condictio after a debtor has been released by acceptilatio mortis causa and the donor later recovers. The transaction is a remission/release of an existing debt as a deathbed gift, not a giving of money or fungibles intended by both parties as a mutuum repayable in kind. Consensus on a loan for consumption is therefore not shown. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
