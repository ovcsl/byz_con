.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio concerning acts of a servus hereditarius who stipulates or receives by delivery. It addresses whose persona gives force to the condictio, namely the deceased/testator’s, not a claim to recover a specific thing as owner. The action is treated as a personal condictio arising from stipulation or receipt, not as a substitute vindication. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.

It addresses from whose legal person the condictio derives its force, namely the deceased/testator, not whether a delivery was intended by both sides as a mutuum. No consensus on a credit repayable in kind is shown. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
