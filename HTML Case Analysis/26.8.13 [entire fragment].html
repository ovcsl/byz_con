.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the explicit condictio for non-debt money paid to impuberes with the tutor’s authority interposed. It concerns pecunia soluta, a determinate monetary payment recoverable by condictio, and the issue is only the minors’ liability despite silence because tutor authority suffices. The claim is therefore for a certum by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
