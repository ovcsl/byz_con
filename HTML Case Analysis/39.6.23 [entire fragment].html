.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the one arising when a mortis causa gift is made to a filius familias and the son dies while the donor lives. The claim lies because the basis of the mortis causa donation has failed, depending on whether the gift was intended for the son or through him for the father. It is a personal restitutionary condictio, not a claim by an owner vindicating a specific thing as still his property. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio scenario: recovery after a mortis causa donation to a filius familias when the son dies while the donor lives. The giving is characterized as a donation, with the issue being whether it was made to the son or through him to the father. There is no shared intention to create a mutuum repayable in kind. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.4.3100Persona alieni iuris (relationship entered into with a slave or son under paternal power)1AktionThe decisive condictio is the statement that, on the son’s death, a condictio lies if the donor gave to the filius familias himself rather than to the father. The recipient is a son under paternal power, and the text contrasts this with the case where the father merely used him as a minister, which would count on the father’s own side. Thus the decisive passage concerns an alieni iuris dependant not acting as the father’s authorised instrument; the criterion is satisfied.
.4.4101Peculium increased and enriched (liability de peculio)0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the mortis causa gift made to a filius familias, with condictio arising on the son’s death if the gift was intended for him rather than for the father. The passage distinguishes whether the son was recipient or merely the father’s minister, and extends the point to a slave, but it does not mention peculium or liability measured de peculio. No enrichment of a separate fund is shown. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
