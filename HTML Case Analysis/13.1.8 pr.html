.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the last explicit condictio scenario: against the thief, after the stolen corpus can no longer be produced, a condictio for its valuation endures. Although the victim was owner, the claim rests on the furtum and is for aestimatio because the thing itself cannot be restored. It is therefore not a vindicatory condictio based on subsisting ownership. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the condictio in respect of a stolen thing, extending to its value if the corpus no longer exists and the thief has not offered it. The passage concerns furtive liability for restitution/aestimatio, not a consensual credit of fungibles to be repaid tantundem. No mutual intention to create a mutuum appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
