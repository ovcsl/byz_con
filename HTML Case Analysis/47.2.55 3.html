.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive passage is the single theft-related scenario contrasting the penal actio furti with condictio and vindicatio for recovery of the thing. The condictio is expressly treated as a means of rei recuperatio and is extinguished once the res is recovered, while it remains after payment of the penalty. This is a furtive-taking context where the stolen thing or its value is pursued as a determinate object by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
