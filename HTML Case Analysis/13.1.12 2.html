.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio: the pledgee sues by incerti condictio when the pledged thing has been stolen. He does not proceed as owner of the thing, but as a pledgee with a personal remedy arising from the furtive taking and his secured interest. A furtive-taking condictio is not grounded in ownership as such. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the pledgee’s incerti condictio when the pledged thing has been stolen. The underlying transaction is a pignus, not a giving of money or fungibles as a mutuum repayable in kind. No coinciding intention to create a loan for consumption appears. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
