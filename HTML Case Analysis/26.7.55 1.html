.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio-passage is the final clause about tutors who have stolen the ward’s thing and whether, besides the duplum action, one could claim the thing itself or its value quasi specie condictionis. This is framed in a theft/tutorship-delict context, not as a vindicatory claim grounded on subsisting ownership. Even if the ward remains owner, the condictio-like claim would rest on the wrongful taking, so the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio-passage is the tutor’s theft of the ward’s thing, where a further recovery 'quasi specie condictionis' of the thing or its value is denied alongside the duplum action. This is not a consensual giving of fungibles as a mutuum, but a wrongful handling/theft within tutela. There is no coinciding intention to create credit repayable in kind. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
