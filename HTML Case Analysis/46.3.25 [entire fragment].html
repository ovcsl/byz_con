.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the part-heir who paid the whole ten promised by the deceased and seeks to condict the portion not owed by him. This is a personal condictio indebiti for an overpaid amount, later barred by exceptio doli when the remaining inheritance accrues. It is not a claim to reclaim a specific thing as subsisting owner. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the part-heir who pays the whole sum promised by the deceased and seeks to condict the excess not corresponding to his hereditary share. The giving is framed as performance of a debt, and later as an alleged indebitum, not as money advanced on credit to be repaid in kind. There is no coinciding intention of mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the partial heir who paid the whole ten promised by the deceased and seeks by condictio the excess corresponding to the non-inherited share, with a later denial by exceptio doli if that share accrues to him before suit. The object is a determinate sum: decem, or a fixed fractional part of that sum. This is recovery of a certum by condictio indebiti, so the criterion is satisfied.
