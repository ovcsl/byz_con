.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the final one: if the mortis causa donor recovers, he may by condictio or an actio in factum receive back only the debtor’s obligation. The object discussed is not a fixed sum of money or a specifically identified corporeal thing, but the restitution of an obligation as such, valued by the debtor’s solvency. This does not show a certum pursued by condictio. Conclusion: not satisfied.
