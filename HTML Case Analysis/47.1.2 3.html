.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the single condictio ex causa furtiva scenario, asking whether Aquilian liability may also be pursued after a furtive condictio. This condictio is grounded in the theft-delict and its special valuation, not in an assertion that the claimant vindicates the very thing as owner. .7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single condictio ex causa furtiva, restated in comparison with the Aquilian action. In Roman doctrine this condictio pursues the stolen thing or its value as a determinate object, with valuation fixed at the time of litis contestatio rather than by the Aquilian yearly maximum. Thus a certum is claimable by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
