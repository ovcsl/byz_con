.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the single payment of indebiti nummi by the procurator, with possible condictiones by the principal ex causa furtiva and by the payer upon ratification. The claim is framed as a condictio arising from theft/undue payment, not as recovery of specific coins on the basis of subsisting ownership. It is therefore a personal restitutionary/delictal claim, not a vindicatory substitute. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the procurator’s undue payment of his own coins, with condictio discussed for the procurator ex causa furtiva and for the principal upon ratification. The giving is treated as an indebitum solutum, not as credit granted to be repaid in the same kind and quantity. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the single transaction in which a procurator pays his own undue coins and the knowing recipient is liable ex causa furtiva, with competing condictiones of procurator and ratifying principal. The object is the paid coins, i.e. a determinate monetary object/value, and the text expressly discusses condicere ex causa furtiva. This satisfies the certum element.
