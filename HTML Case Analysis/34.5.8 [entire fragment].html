.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the surviving donor’s ability to condicere the thing donated between spouses when the donee dies first. The passage treats the claim as restitution of a thing transferred under an interspousal donation, with the donation’s validity depending on survival, not as a vindicatory assertion that the donor remains owner. Thus the condictio is not grounded in subsisting ownership of the specific thing. Conclusion: not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single express condictio: the surviving donor would be able to condicere rem, i.e. reclaim the donated thing. The object is the donated res itself, treated as a specific thing recoverable by condictio, not as an incertum. The passage does not rest the remedy on vindication from continued ownership, but on the donor’s personal recovery after the marital donation fails. Thus the certum element is satisfied.
