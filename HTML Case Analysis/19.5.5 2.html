.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the last explicit one: after something is given so that the recipient manumits a slave, and the time for manumission has passed, the giver may condicere or sue praescriptis verbis. The claim arises from failure of the agreed counter-performance in a do ut facias transaction, not from subsisting ownership of a specific thing reclaimed as his property. It is a personal restitutionary condictio, not a vindicatory substitute. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the last explicit one: a thing is given so that the recipient manumits a slave, and after sufficient time condici ei potest or praescriptis verbis agi. This is a do ut facias exchange for manumission, not a giving intended by both parties as a mutuum repayable in the same kind and quantity. Consensus on a loan for consumption is therefore absent.
