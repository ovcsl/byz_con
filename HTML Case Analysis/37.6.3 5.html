.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the explicit condictio where the emancipated son, after paying money for collation, seeks to recover it because he did not pursue bonorum possessio. The claim is grounded in the money being sine causa apud heredem after the procedural basis failed, i.e. restitution of a payment/enrichment. It is not framed as reclaiming a specific thing on subsisting ownership. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the emancipated son who actually contributed money for bonorum collatio and then repeats it by condictio after omitting bonorum possessio. The payment is made as collation in the inheritance context, not as a credit intended by both parties to be repaid in tantundem. No mutual intention of mutuum appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
