.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: “domino condictio competet,” with only one of two possible actors allowed to sue. It identifies the claimant as dominus, but does not show that he reclaims a subsisting specific thing as owner or that the condictio replaces a vindicatio. The ground of liability is not stated as ownership itself, so the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: the owner too has a condictio, with only one of two persons permitted to sue. It states entitlement to the remedy but gives no facts showing a loan for consumption or coinciding intentions to create a mutuum. The parties’ intentions cannot be determined, so the criterion is not satisfied.
