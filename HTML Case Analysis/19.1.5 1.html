.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is Pomponius’ statement that the person who promised under a false belief that he was condemned to give may condicere incerti, ut liberetur. The claim seeks release from an obligation, not recovery of a specific thing as his continuing property. It is therefore a personal remedial claim, not a condictio standing in place of vindication. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is Pomponius’ statement that the promisor may bring a condictio incerti to be released from an obligation assumed under the false belief that he had been condemned to give. The passage concerns a sale or stipulatio-like promise and liberation from that obligation, not a giving of money or fungibles intended by both parties as a mutuum. No consensus on a loan for consumption is shown. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
