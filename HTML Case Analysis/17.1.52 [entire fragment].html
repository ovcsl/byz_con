This is a personal recourse claim arising from the surety’s performance, limited by what the principal could have used to discharge the debt. .7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: the fideiussor seeks to condicere from the debtor the better wheat which he paid to the creditor. The object is wheat owed under a stipulation, with the dispute concerning the quality recoverable; it is not an ownership vindication or a defective-credit case. The condictio claim concerns a determinate prestation of wheat, though barred by dolus beyond the worst quality due. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
