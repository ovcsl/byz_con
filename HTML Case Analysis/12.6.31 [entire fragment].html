.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the single passage granting an indebiti promissi condictio to one who, by mistake, promised a creditor more than his hereditary share. The claim concerns release or restitution of an undue promise, i.e. a personal obligation arising from error, not recovery of a specific thing as continuing owner. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the heir who, by error, gives a cautio/promissory undertaking to a creditor for more than his hereditary share. This concerns an undue stipulatory promise to a creditor, not a giving of money or fungibles as a mutuum repayable in kind. No coinciding intention to create a loan for consumption appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio: an heir who by mistake has promised a creditor more than his hereditary share has an indebiti promissi condictio. The excess promised to the creditor is a determinate monetary obligation, ascertainable from the hereditary portion and the debt, and the passage concerns undoing an undue stipulatory promise for that certum. Thus the certum element is satisfied.
