.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the last scenario: the debtor himself has paid the creditor either by actio furti or by condictio after the pledged thing was stolen. That condictio is tied to the furtive taking and the resulting personal liability, not to the creditor reclaiming a specific thing as owner. It therefore does not stand in place of a vindication. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the last one: the debtor himself has paid the creditor by condictio in relation to the stolen pledge. This concerns liability arising from the pledge/theft context and its imputation to the debt, not a giving of fungibles intended by both parties as a mutuum. The parties’ consensus toward a loan for consumption is not shown. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
