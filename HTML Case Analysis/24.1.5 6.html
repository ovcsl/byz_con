.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: after divorce, the spouse may condicere where the servitude was not used donationis causa and was thereby lost. The claim is not based on subsisting ownership of a specific thing reclaimed as one’s own; the servitude is expressly said to have been lost. It is a personal restitutionary claim arising from the marital donation context, not a vindicatory condictio. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the claim after divorce where a spouse, donationis causa, refrained from using a servitude and thereby lost it. The transaction is framed as a prohibited marital donation concerning a servitude, not as money or fungibles advanced to be repaid in kind. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
