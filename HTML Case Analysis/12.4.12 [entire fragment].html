.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the donor’s claim after recovery to recover what was given mortis causa, including fruits, offspring, and accessions. The claim rests on the failure of the mortis causa basis of the transfer, not on an assertion that the donated things remained the donor’s property. This is a personal restitutionary condictio, not a vindicatory claim in disguise. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

There is no indication that both parties intended a mutuum or repayment of tantundem. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

The passage concerns restitution of the donated things, with fruits, offspring, and accretions as accessories to those identifiable things. This is recovery of determinate donated objects by condictio. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
