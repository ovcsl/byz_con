.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the first alternative: ten are given to Titius solely so that he buy Stichus, and after Stichus dies the giver repeats them by condictio. This is a personal restitutionary claim for the sum given on failure of the intended causa, not a vindicatory claim to a specific thing as still owned by the claimant. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the variant where ten are given to Titius only so that he buy Stichus, and Stichus dies before purchase, so the giver repeats by condictio. The giving is purpose-bound and conditional, not a credit intended by both parties to be repaid as tantundem. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

The object pursued is a fixed sum, decem, hence a certum; the issue is failure of the stated purpose, not ownership or incapacity. Therefore the criterion is satisfied.
