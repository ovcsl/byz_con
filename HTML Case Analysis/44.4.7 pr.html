.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio is the claim against the stipulator after the claimant, mistakenly thinking he owed money to the addressee, promised it by stipulation to a third party. The relief sought is liberation from a personal stipulatory obligation, not recovery of a specific thing as the claimant’s subsisting property. This is an enrichment/restitutionary personal claim, not a condictio standing in place of vindication. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.

No mutual intention to create a mutuum appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: the mistaken promisor has a condictio against the stipulator “ut me liberet.” Although the underlying stipulation concerned money, the condictio asserted here is not to recover a fixed sum or identified thing, but to obtain release from the stipulatory obligation. Thus the passage does not show a certum claimed by condictio. Conclusion: not satisfied.
