.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the fuller’s possible condictio against the owner for the price he paid after the clothes were lost and later found. The claim is for recovery of a payment treated as quasi sine causa, not for reclaiming a specific thing as the fuller’s property. It is a personal restitutionary claim for money, so the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the fuller’s payment of the value of the lost garments to the owner, followed by the garments being found. That payment is treated as possibly recoverable quasi sine causa, not as money advanced on credit to be repaid in the same amount and kind. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
