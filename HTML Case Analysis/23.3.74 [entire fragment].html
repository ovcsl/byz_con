.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the sponsa who gave a dowry but no valid marriage followed, so recovery is allowed exemplo dotis condictioni. The text expressly treats this as a personal action with a privilege, not as reclamation of a specific thing on the basis of subsisting ownership. It is restitution of a dowry-like transfer, not a condictio standing in place of vindication. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the dowry-like condictio where a sponsa gave a dos but no valid marriage followed. The giving is treated as dowry or by analogy to dowry, not as money or fungibles advanced as a mutuum to be repaid in tantundem. There is no coinciding intention of giver and recipient to create a loan for consumption. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
