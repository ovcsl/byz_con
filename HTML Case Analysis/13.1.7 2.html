.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the condictio rei furtivae against the thief’s heir, including the statement that the condictio endures even after the stolen slave’s death. Although the owner seeks recovery connected with his stolen thing, the claim is grounded in theft and the personal liability of the thief and successors, not in a vindicatory assertion of subsisting ownership. Since the thing may even have ceased to exist, this is not reclaiming the specific thing as owner. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
