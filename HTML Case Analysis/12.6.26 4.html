.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio: the debtor, having mistakenly paid a fundus worth 200 for a debt of 100, has a condictio for the whole land while the original obligation remains. The claim arises from mistaken overpayment in performance of an obligation, not from an assertion that the land remains his property. Since the fundus was delivered as payment and the remedy is personal restitution by condictio, not a vindicatory claim based on ownership, the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the mistaken payment of a fundus worth 200 by a debtor who in fact owed only 100. The parties are treating the transfer as performance of an existing stipulation, not as a mutuum or credit repayable in the same kind and quantity. No consensus toward a loan for consumption appears; the criterion is not satisfied.
