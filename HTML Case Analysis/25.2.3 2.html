.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio scenario: a furtiva condictio against a wife who committed theft against the claimant or his predecessor. Although the stolen thing belonged to the victim, the text grants the condictio as the substitute for the barred actio furti because of marital reverence. Its basis is the delict of theft, not an assertion of subsisting ownership vindicating the specific thing. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio-passage is the single statement that, in both theft cases involving the wife, only the furtive condictio is available and not the actio furti. A condictio furtiva pursues the stolen thing or its value as a determinate object arising ex causa furtiva. Thus the passage shows a certum claimable by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
