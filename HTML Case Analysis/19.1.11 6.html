.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the final one: a ring given as arra, after completion of the sale and payment, is not returned and is said to be condicible because it is now sine causa with the seller. The claim rests on failure/cessation of the causa for retaining the ring, not on an assertion that the buyer sues as owner by a vindicatory substitute. It is therefore a personal restitutionary condictio, not an ownership-based recovery. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the ring given as arrha in a sale, later retained by the seller after the sale was completed and the price paid. The giving is treated as earnest connected with the sale, not as money or fungibles credited to be repaid in tantundem. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

The text expressly says it can be condicted because it is now sine causa with the seller. The object is a determinate identified thing, the anulus, so the certum element is satisfied.
