.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the general statement that something may be condicted if it came to the defendant without a just cause or later falls back into an unjust cause. It frames condictio by absence or failure of causa, not by the claimant’s subsisting ownership of a specific thing. This is personal restitution/enrichment logic, not a vindicatory substitute. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive passage is the general condici statement about what may be recovered when something came to a person without a just cause or the cause later became unjust. It does not describe any giving as a mutuum, nor any coinciding intentions of giver and recipient to create a credit repayable in kind. The required consensus for a loan for consumption is therefore not shown.
