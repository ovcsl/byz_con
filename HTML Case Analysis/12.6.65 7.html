.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive passage is the explicit claim 'habitatione data pecuniam condicam', where money is condicted for the value of habitation supplied. The claimant does not reclaim a specific thing as his subsisting property, but seeks a monetary measure of enrichment or use-value. This is a personal claim for money, not a condictio standing in place of vindication. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: after habitation has been provided, money is claimed by condictio measured by what the recipient would have paid to rent it. This concerns restitution/value for use of habitation, not a credit of money or fungibles to be repaid in kind. No coinciding intention to create a mutuum appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
