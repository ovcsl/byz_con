.180Condictio ex mutuo1Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the lender’s condictio against the dominus or pater after money is counted to the slave or son by their order as a mutuum. The claim arises from the loan obligation and seeks repayment of the amount lent, not recovery of specific coins as the claimant’s subsisting property. Thus the claimant proceeds as creditor, not as owner in place of vindication. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)1Logik
.1.2.168Consensus1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the counting of money to a slave or son at the order of the master or father, followed by condici against the master or father himself. The text expressly frames the money as pecunia mutua acceptura, so the giving is intended as a mutuum despite payment being made to the subordinate. The parties’ intentions coincide on a credit repayable in kind. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
.1.2.270Datio (transfer of ownership)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the money lent by mutuum to the master or father, with numeratio made to the slave or son on his order, for which condici possit against the master or father himself. The text expressly frames the transaction as pecuniam mutuam accepturus, and delivery to the dependent person functions as delivery to the paterfamilias/dominus for acquisition. This is a concrete mutuum datio, not a mere payment under another transaction. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
.1.389Mutuum claim at issue (the loan itself is the doctrine under discussion)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the explicit statement that money lent is paid, at the paterfamilias’ or dominus’ order, to a son or slave, and can be condicted from the paterfamilias or dominus himself. The point is the formation of the mutuum despite numeratio to a third party at the borrower’s direction, not a failed stipulation or merely abstract enrichment. Thus the mutuum claim as such is being asserted.
