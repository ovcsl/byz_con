.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive passage is the last scenario: where the son was not a debtor, the surety who nevertheless paid is said to be able to bring a condictio. The condictio is tied to recovery of what was paid under a non-existent debt, i.e. a determinate payment rather than an incertum or a de peculio/de in rem verso claim. Thus a certum is claimable by condictio.
