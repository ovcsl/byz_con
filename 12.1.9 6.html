.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio where money was counted out and its return was arranged by a stipulation subject to an impossible condition. The text treats the condictio as remaining because the stipulation is void, not because both parties intended a mutuum repayable in kind. Under Roman reasoning this is a numeratio tied to a stipulation, not shown as consensual credit. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the explicit condictio after money has been counted out and an accompanying stipulation is made under an impossible condition. The stipulation is void, but the numeratio remains as the basis for recovery by condictio. This is a determinate monetary credit claimable by condictio, so the criterion is satisfied.
