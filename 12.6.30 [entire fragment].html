No coinciding intention to create a loan for consumption appears. Conclusion: not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)0AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio: a reciprocal creditor-debtor who pays despite a counterclaim, where compensation is unavailable, is denied a condictio veluti indebiti soluti. The text discusses a payment and substitutes pursuit of the payer’s own credit, but it does not identify a fixed sum or specific thing as the object recoverable by that condictio. Thus the certum element is not shown.
