.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive passage is the single explicit condictio: the promissor has a condictio against the woman to recover what he paid to the husband, or to be released if he has not yet paid. The object is indebitam pecuniam, i.e. a fixed monetary performance, and the text discusses its recovery by condictio. This is a determinate pecuniary claim, not an incertum or ownership-based claim. Therefore the criterion is satisfied.
