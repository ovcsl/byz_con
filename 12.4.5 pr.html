.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the giving of money so that the recipient will travel to Capua, with the last passage allowing condictio only for the excess after necessary expenses. This is a personal restitutionary claim for money given for a purpose that may fail or be renounced, adjusted by the recipient’s indemnity. It is not based on reclaiming a specific thing as the claimant’s subsisting property. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the final one: money was given so the recipient would travel to Capua, and if he spent less than he received, condictio lies subject to reimbursement of his expenses. The giving is tied to a purpose or causa, not to a consensual credit repayable as tantundem eiusdem generis. No mutual intention to create a mutuum appears. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
