.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the single case of a transfer made so that the recipient manumit Stichus, with condictio if he does not do so or if the giver repents. The claim rests on failure or withdrawal of the causa of the delivery, not on the claimant asserting continuing ownership of a specific thing. This is a personal restitutionary condictio, not a vindicatory substitute. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the giving to the recipient so that he manumit Stichus, with condictio if he fails or if the giver repents. The parties’ purpose is performance of a manumission, not a credit of fungibles to be returned in the same kind and quantity. There is no consensus on a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
