.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the pupil who, without tutor’s authority, lends or pays money, and after the money is consumed has a condictio against the recipient. The claim is not a vindicatory recovery of the same specific coins as still owned; the money has been consumed and the liability is personal, arising from credit/payment and enrichment. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the ward who, without tutor’s authority, gives money credendi causa, alongside the payment variant. The credit variant expressly treats the giving as in creditum/crediderit, so giver and recipient coincide in intending a mutuum-like credit; the lack of tutor’s auctoritas concerns capacity/transfer, not consensus. Therefore the criterion is satisfied.
.1.2.270Datio (transfer of ownership)0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the ward who, without tutor’s authority, gives money either as credit or in payment and, after consumption, has a condictio. A pupillus acting sine tutoris auctoritate lacks the capacity/authority to alienate so ownership is not treated as passing by a valid datio; liability arises only because the money was consumed and thereby reached the recipient’s patrimony. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
