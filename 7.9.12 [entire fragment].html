.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the owner’s possible condictio for the vessels delivered to the usufructuary while ownership remains with him. The text expressly denies such a condictio: one cannot condict his own thing except from a thief. The only stated exception is furtum, where the condictio rests on the delict, not on ownership as such. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the question whether the owner can bring a condictio for vessels delivered to the legatee for usufruct. The text states that they were delivered only fruendi causa, not so that ownership should pass, and the recipient is to use and enjoy the same vessels. This is not a mutually intended mutuum or credit repayable by tantundem; the criterion is not satisfied.

Although the vessels are specific things, the claim is framed precisely as one by the continuing owner for his own property, and the text says one cannot condicere one’s own thing except from a thief. This falls under the continued-ownership exclusion. Conclusion: not satisfied.
