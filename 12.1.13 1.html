.180Condictio ex mutuo0Logik
.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the partial condictio after alien coins delivered as a mutuum have been consumed. The claim arises only after consumption and is for the corresponding amount, not for recovery of the specific coins as the claimant’s subsisting property. Since the things no longer exist in specie and the action is personal, the criterion is not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single condictio concerning alien coins given as a mutuum and consumed in parts. The phrase “alienos nummos tibi mutuos dedi” shows the giving was treated as a loan for consumption; the fact that the coins belonged to another affects datio/ownership, not the parties’ credit intention. Thus the required consensus for a mutuum is present.
.1.2.270Datio (transfer of ownership)0AktionThe decisive scenario is the explicit condictio for alien coins given as a mutuum and consumed in parts. The passage expressly says the coins were another’s, so the giver lacked ownership and authority to alienate them. Liability arises only after consumption, not because ownership passed from the giver by datio. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
