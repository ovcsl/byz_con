.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the possible condicere for a thing that has been subtracted or stolen, alongside ad exhibendum and vindicatio. Although the claimant may be owner, the condictio here is tied to the furtive taking and the praetor’s denial of actions arising from it, not to ownership as the juridical ground replacing vindication. Therefore the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the single discussion whether the praetor’s denial after something has been subtracted also bars the victim from condicere. This concerns a furtive taking, and the condictio would pursue the stolen thing or its value as a determinate object, not merely an incertum. Therefore the criterion is satisfied.
