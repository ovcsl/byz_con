.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive explicit condictio is the seller who, ignorant of his perpetual defence, delivers the land and is said to be able to condicere the fundus. The claim arises because the delivery was not owed after the heir was charged to release him, not because the seller sues as continuing owner by a vindicatory substitute. The land was delivered as performance to an acquirer, so the condictio is personal restitution, not ownership-based. Conclusion: not satisfied.
.1.25Contract mutuum (contract loan for consumption)0Logik
.1.2.168Consensus0AktionThe decisive explicit condictio scenario is the vendor who, unaware that he has been released from the sale obligation, delivers the land and is said to be able to condicere the fundus. This concerns restitution after an ineffective or no-longer-owed transfer in a sale context, not a credit of fungibles repayable tantundem. There is no coinciding intention to create a mutuum. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.
