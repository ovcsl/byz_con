.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single explicit condictio: recovery of the usufruct of the giver’s own land, granted by mistake as if owed. The object is the specifically identified usufruct of a particular fundus, not an incertum, and the claim rests on mistaken performance rather than ownership. Thus a determinate object is claimable by condictio. Conclusion: satisfied.
