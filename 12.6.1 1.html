.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive scenario is the single indebitum-payment case: one who unknowingly pays what is not owed may condicere, while knowing payment bars repetition. The condictio concerns recovery of the thing or sum paid as the undue payment, i.e. a determinate object arising from solutio/numeratio. Nothing indicates an incertum claim or a defective-credit constellation. Conclusion: satisfied.
