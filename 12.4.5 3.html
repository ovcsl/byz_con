.1.181Plaintiff is no owner1Logik
.1.1.112Plaintiff is owner0AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the money accepted so that the recipient would manumit a slave, with the question whether what was accepted can be condicted after the slave fled. The claim is for restitution of the payment or its equivalent because the intended causa failed or became disputed, not because the giver asserts ownership of a specific thing. This is a personal restitutionary claim, not a condictio standing in place of vindicatio. Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

Conclusion: the criterion is not satisfied.

.7.393Ex qua certum petitur (numeratio)1AktionThe decisive condictio scenario is the single case of money accepted so that a particular slave be manumitted, with the slave fleeing before manumission. The text expressly asks whether what was received can be condicted and discusses restitution of the accepted sum, later identified as ten. That is a determinate monetary claim by condictio, not an incertum or ownership-based claim. Conclusion: the criterion is satisfied.
